AirAssist V1.4 front-end service design refinement
Date: 2026-10-09 Pacific/Auckland
Scope: public/index.html, public/app.js and public/styles.css.
No backend, personal SAP configuration, database, credentials, deployment, presentation or source test changes.

User intent
The user approved the broad page direction but asked for a less generic AI-looking interface. The refinement follows an airline service pattern: clear booking and application tasks, compact navigation, calm typography and visible saved state, with fewer nested panels and repeated developer-facing labels.

Implemented changes
1. Use a white page, restrained teal/charcoal accents, Arial/Helvetica-family typography, readable field labels and a consistent small-radius control system. Existing brand, masthead navigation, data and actions remain.
2. Replace outer lookup cards, nested rounded staff cards and full-width shaded routing panels with thin horizontal separators. Reduce uppercase kicker labels and decorative numbered circles. The staff review task and saved next step are visually distinct without a dashboard of cards.
3. Remove the homepage's duplicate traveller card. The traveller remains in the itinerary and form summary; route, booking channel, date, ticket value, source, refund uncertainty and ticket details remain available.
4. Change visible task labels to normal service language: Manage your booking, Review a refund request, Case review, Review the evidence, Save review, Reopen review, Review history and Saved next step. IDs, form names, data attributes, API calls, state guards and actions are unchanged.
5. Consolidate the general app identity to Course prototype in the masthead and a plain footer describing sample bookings and local request storage. Retain the essential boundaries at decision points: no cancellation or payment, local save/queue only, synthetic ticket values, generic SAP reference with booking/payment/eligibility unknowns, no automatically completed evidence checks.
6. Passenger reasons use normal stacked form rows, not three decorative tiles. Booking summary and refund-action sidebars use a simple divider. Request tracking prioritises saved progress and dates with plain pending labels.
7. On mobile, retain task-first ordering, full-width fields, 16px input text, 48px action/disclosure/shortcut targets, bottom navigation clearance, native keyboard disclosures, visible focus, reduced motion and unsaved draft recovery.
8. After the root-agent slide review identified a disabled Send control too similar to its enabled outline state, give all disabled primary/outline buttons a clearly grey-filled background, grey border and readable grey text. Disabled hover has the same grey appearance; enabled controls retain their white/teal styling. The disabled attribute and all guard logic are unchanged.
