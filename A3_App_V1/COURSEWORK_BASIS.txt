Rationale: why this increment and what it can demonstrate
The prototype targets case readiness before an informed human decision. A traveler supplies a traceable local application; the reviewer sees the known application and synthetic journey together, records missing manual checks, and chooses a justified standard/specialist pathway. Saved route/queue/role and chronological events persist and appear in passenger tracking. This is a closer functional representation of A2's preparation/routing/ownership mechanism than the historical V1 request/receipt alone. The application cannot replace authorized airline records, staff business-rule validation, payment controls or production workflow integration.
The effort/impact comparison uses explicit qualitative criteria. Potential impact means reduced preventable retrieval/exchanges, clearer ownership and better traceability without unsafe decisions. Effort means interfaces/data access, validated routing rules, state/security controls, testing and operational change. There are no measured effort hours, monetary costs, realized savings or customer satisfaction scores.
