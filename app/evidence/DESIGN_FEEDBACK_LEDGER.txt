Define
The scoped service helps demonstrate a selected direct-booking ticket-refund request becoming traceable and ready for human review. For this learning prototype, synthetic itinerary/request data and a generic SAP financial-document lookup are separate. The prototype cannot claim full authorized internal record reconciliation or a verified financial link to Air New Zealand.
Eligibility, refund amount, approval and payment stay under human authority. Meal/hotel/taxi disruption reimbursement and whole-airline system replacement are outside scope.

Ideate
The main task compared the initial visual direction with a task-focused airline service layout. API fit is treated as Option 2: retain refund scope and the existing TO-BE, but modify the wrapper and demonstrated scope around limited read capabilities and explicit provenance. Availability of a SAP read endpoint does not establish a write permission or a completed airline refund.
This ledger records the chosen direction; it does not claim a formal brainstorming workshop or new stakeholder interviews.
